The tittle-tattle is prejudicial, but the winking harms nobody.
This saying means that «Chattering excessively is more prejudicial than an exces-

Se desprezares o colmo curto para cobrir a casa, dormirás ao relento.

If you despise the short thatch, you will sleep in the open air.
