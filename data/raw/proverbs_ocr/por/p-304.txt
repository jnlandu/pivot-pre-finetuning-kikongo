SYA VUVU KU NZO A KO, MALENGE (TULA VUVU KYA DILA KUNZO,
NKINGA MALENGE KALEMBE).
Quem espera comer em casa da sogra, encontra apenas abóbora.
Esperar festejar em casa alheia, traz decepção.
Qui espêre bien manger chez la belle-mêre, ne trouve que des citrouilles.
Espérer fêter chez un autre, amêne déceptions.
He who hopes to eat well at the mother-in-law's home, will find only boiled
gourds.
To pretend to make feast in another's home brings deception.
