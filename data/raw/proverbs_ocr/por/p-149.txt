| A falta de um lugar seguro para dormir é uma disgraça por todo ser vivente.

também a sua mágoa à tardinha.
Le chagrin de la poule arrive le soir.
Le manque d'un endroit sâr pour dormir est une situation affligeante pour tous
les êtres vivants.
Comparés á la poule de ce proverbe, les orphelins, les enfants de la rue, et autres
sans-logis, sentent une tristesse plus poignante que celle de la poule.
The sorrow of the hen comes in the evening.
The lack of a place where to lay one's head is a misfortune for the human beings.
More than the hen referred to in this saying, the orphans, the street children and
other honmeless people, feel more sorrow over their situation every evening.
