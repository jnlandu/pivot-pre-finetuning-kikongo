«Quand mes amis sont borgnes, je les regarde de profil» (J.Joubert).
If you look at an albino, cast down your eyes, because one does
not stare twice at an albino.
This saying advises us not to stare at someone which is different from us, because
our attitude would be perceived as unfriendly and aggressive and would beget a
great repulsion from him/her.
