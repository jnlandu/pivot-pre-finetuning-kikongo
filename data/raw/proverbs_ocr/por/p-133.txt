KUBAKIDI NYOKA VA KATIKO. '
Não apanhes uma serpente no meio.
Este provérbio aconselha de não se intrometer num assunto do qual se desconhe-
ce as entradas e saídas.
N'attrapez pas un serpent en son milieu.
Ce proverbe nous conseille de ne pas nous immiscer dans une affaire dont nous
ignorons les tenants et aboutissants.
Don't catch a snake in its middle.
This saying advises us not to meddle in a matter of which we don't know the ins
and the outs.

Foolness precedes wisdom, or «If I knew» comes always late,
This proverb refers to all people who reflect only after having wronged as illus-
trated by La Fontaine in the conclusion of his fable «The Crow and the Fox»:
