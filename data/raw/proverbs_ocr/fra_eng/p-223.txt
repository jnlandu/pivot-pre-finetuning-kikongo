It is better to bring forth one’s own child than to bring up another’s child, and
it is more useful to deal with your own money than to rely on another's.
This saying means that your own child is a secure guarantee for you than a step-
son and that the money earned by yourself is more guaranteed than another's.
However, there are sons and daughters who are indifferent and shirk helping
their parents while anothers’ sons feel grateful to those from whom they received
some favours in their life.
This shows that the exception confirms the rule.
