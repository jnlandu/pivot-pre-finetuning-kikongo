Le sage Salomon nous avertit: «La boisson volée est agréable et la nourriture

Where you have never been, the sky seems to be united with the earth.
Things unknown look like mirages,

All that is unknown ou prohibited is pleasing or delightul to look at.
The wise Salomon warns us: «Stolen water is sweeter. Stolen bread tastes better»

KUNA KUYENGANGA NZUNDU, TADI ULALANGA KO.
Onde tinem o martelo e a bigorna, gasta-se o ferro.
Todo grande trabalho de longa duraçäo consome tamanha energia, torna-se des-
gastante e acarreta sacrificios.
Où tintent le marteau et l’enclume, le fer s’use.
Tout grand travail de longue haleine consomme une grande énergie, qui usent les
corps en présence et exige d’énormes sacrifices.
| Where the hammer and the anvil are clinking, the iron decreases.
All long and exacting labour to which the workers devote their energies weakens
| their bodies and minds in the long run. All men who devote their life to a great
cause accept and make great sacrifices.
