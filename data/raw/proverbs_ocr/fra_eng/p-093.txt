If we go forwards we'll die; if we go backwards we'll die. -
It is better then to go forwards even if we must face death.
The world is moving ahead and doesn’t go backwards. So to die going forwards is
a progress while to die going backwards is a regression. In both cases of an
implacable death, it is wiser «to choose» that which represents progress and
therefore the «lesser of the two evils». John Bunyan peremptorily confirmed it
when he said: «To go back is nothing but death; to go forward is fear of death,
and life everlasting beyond it: I will yet go forward» (The Pilgrim’s Progress).
