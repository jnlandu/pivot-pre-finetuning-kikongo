KYAKU KYAKU, KYANGANI KYANGANI.

«O teu bem é seguro, o de outrem no o é para ti».

Votre bien vous est garanti, le bien d’autrui ne l’est pas pour vous,
What belongs to you is yours, what belongs to another man is not yours.
You can rely on your belongings and not on those of the others.
