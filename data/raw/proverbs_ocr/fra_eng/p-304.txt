SYA VUVU KU NZO A KO, MALENGE (TULA VUVU KYA DILA KUNZO,
NKINGA MALENGE KALEMBE).
Quem espera comer em casa da sogra, encontra apenas abdbora.
Esperar festejar em casa alheia, traz decepçäo.
Qui espére bien manger chez la belle-mére, ne trouve que des citrouilles.
Espérer féter chez un autre, améne déceptions.
He who hopes to eat well at the mother-in-law’s home, will find only boiled
gourds.
To pretend to make feast in another’s home brings deception.
