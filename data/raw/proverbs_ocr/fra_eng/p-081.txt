Humilie-toi, sans cependant te mépriser.
«L'humilité précède la gloire» (Provérbios 15:33).
«Qui s'élève sera abaissé, et qui s‘abaissa sera élevé» (Lucas 14:11).
If you are intelligent, don’t be vainglorious, because intelligent people are
legion. Hence, humiliate you without however debasing yourself.
«Humility precedes glory» (Proverbs 15:33).
«Everyone who makes himself great will be humbled, and everyone who hum-
bles himself will be made great» (Luke 14:11).
